WHAT WE DO — 03

Data & Analytics

We transform operational data into structured, accessible and actionable information.

OVERVIEW

Data only helps when people can use it.

Organizations generate data every day — in sales, stock, finance, operations and customer service. But when that data is scattered across systems and files, it is hard to trust and harder to use.

Baryon builds the pipelines, structures and views that turn raw operational data into information people can read, question and act on — treating data as a strategic asset rather than a by-product.

What it covers

Seven areas of work under Data & Analytics.

01

Data Integration

Bringing data from separate systems, spreadsheets and sources into one consistent view.

02

Data Engineering

Building the pipelines and storage that collect, clean and structure data reliably and repeatably.

03

Business Intelligence

Turning structured data into reports and views that show how the business is performing.

04

Analytics Platforms

Environments where teams can explore data, ask questions and track measures over time.

05

Executive Dashboards

Clear, focused views of the indicators that matter most to leadership.

06

Data Management

Practices and tooling for data quality, access and governance across its lifecycle.

07

Decision Support Systems

Systems that combine data and analysis to help people weigh options and act with confidence.

How we work

A practical sequence, adapted to each organization.

01

Identify the questions

We start from the decisions the organization needs to make, not from the data available.

02

Connect the sources

Data from operational systems and files is integrated and standardized.

03

Structure and store

Pipelines clean and organize data so it is reliable and ready for analysis.

04

Present and act

Reports, dashboards and decision-support tools put the information in front of the right people.

What organizations aim to gain

A clearer view

Leadership and teams see how the business is actually performing.

Trusted numbers

Consistent definitions and clean pipelines reduce disagreements over data.

Ready for intelligence

Well-structured data is the base that predictive models and AI build on.

IN THE BARYON APPROACH

Data & Analytics is the "Understand" stage of the Baryon approach: turning operational data into information, analytics and measurable insight.
