WHAT WE DO — 01

Enterprise Software

We build digital systems that help organizations manage and scale their operations — from core business management to software shaped around a specific sector.

OVERVIEW

Software should fit the organization, not the other way around.

Many organizations run on a mix of spreadsheets, paper processes and tools that do not talk to each other. Enterprise software replaces that patchwork with systems that hold the organization's information in one place and support the way its teams actually work.

Baryon designs and builds these systems with African operating conditions in mind — varied connectivity, diverse devices and the practical realities of the teams who use them every day.

What it covers

Seven areas of work under Enterprise Software.

01

Business Management Systems

Central systems that bring day-to-day activities — planning, tracking and reporting — into one place, so every team works from the same information.

02

ERP Solutions

Enterprise resource planning that connects finance, procurement, inventory, sales and operations inside a single system instead of separate tools.

03

CRM Platforms

Platforms to manage customer relationships, sales pipelines and follow-up, giving teams a shared view of every customer interaction.

04

HR Systems

Digital management of employee records, roles, attendance, leave and workforce information.

05

Inventory & Operations Management

Tracking of stock, assets and operational workflows, reducing manual counting and blind spots across sites and teams.

06

Industry-Specific Software

Applications built around the workflows of a particular sector — such as healthcare, mining or logistics — rather than generic templates.

07

Custom Enterprise Applications

Purpose-built software for the cases where off-the-shelf tools do not match how an organization really operates.

How we work

A practical sequence, adapted to each organization.

01

Understand the operation

We map workflows, roles, data and pain points before any design begins.

02

Design for local reality

Interfaces, offline behavior and data flows are shaped around real users, devices and connectivity.

03

Build and integrate

Systems are developed in stages and connected to the tools and data the organization already relies on.

04

Support and evolve

We keep improving the system as the organization grows, adds sites or changes how it works.

What organizations aim to gain

One source of truth

Teams work from the same, current information instead of parallel spreadsheets.

Less manual work

Repetitive entry, re-typing and reconciliation give way to structured workflows.

Room to grow

Foundations designed to extend from one team or site to regional operations.
