1. Introduction

Baryon ("Baryon", "we", "us" or "our") is an African enterprise technology company building software, digital systems, data platforms and AI solutions for organizations across Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect information when you visit our website, use our products, or otherwise interact with us.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, company, email address, phone number and message content when you contact us, request a demo or apply for a partnership.
  • Usage information — such as pages visited, time spent on the site, browser type, device type and general location, collected automatically through standard web technologies.
  • Product and account information — where you use a Baryon product (such as Baryon Operations, Baryon AI or MIDE AI), information related to your organization's use of that product, subject to the specific terms agreed for that product.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested information about our solutions;
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website and products;
  • Communicate with you about updates, partnerships or opportunities relevant to you;
  • Understand how our website and products are used so we can make them more useful; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who support our operations (for example, hosting, analytics or communication tools), under confidentiality obligations;
  • Professional advisors, where necessary for legal, accounting or compliance purposes;
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of Baryon or others; and
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition or restructuring of our business.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to us.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the site.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. International Transfers

As an organization operating across Africa, information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure information continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business use and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle information, contact us at hello@baryon.africa or write to us in Kampala, Uganda.
